YouPiper Helper
===============

WHAT IT DOES

  Lets YouPiper save videos straight to this PC instead of routing them
  through a server, so downloads are faster and are not limited by anyone
  else's bandwidth.


INSTALL

  Double-click Install.cmd.

  That is the whole setup. From then on it starts by itself whenever you
  sign in and stays out of your way - no window, no taskbar button.

  Then just open YouPiper in your browser. It finds the Helper on its own
  and downloads go directly to your Downloads folder.

  Installing needs no administrator rights and changes nothing outside your
  own user account.


IF WINDOWS SHOWS A WARNING

  "Windows protected your PC" appears for downloads that are new or not yet
  widely installed. Choose "More info" then "Run anyway" if you trust this
  download. Your antivirus may also want to scan the files first, which is
  normal and worth letting it do.


TO REMOVE IT

  Double-click Uninstall.cmd.
  Your downloaded files are never touched.


WHAT IT INCLUDES

  YouPiper Helper bundles everything it needs, so you do not have to
  install anything else. See the Licenses folder for the third-party
  software it includes and the licences that cover it.


PRIVACY AND NETWORK

  The Helper listens only on this computer's own loopback interface, on a
  single port. Nothing on your network or the internet can reach it. It
  does not create an account, collect analytics or send any usage data.
