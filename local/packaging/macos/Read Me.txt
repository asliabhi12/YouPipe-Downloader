YouPiper Helper
===============

WHAT IT DOES

  Lets YouPiper save videos straight to this Mac instead of routing them
  through a server, so downloads are faster and are not limited by anyone
  else's bandwidth.


INSTALL

  1. Drag "YouPiper Helper" onto the Applications folder in this window.
  2. Open it once from Applications.

  That is the whole setup. From then on it starts by itself whenever you
  log in and stays out of your way — no window, no Dock icon, no menu.

  Then just open YouPiper in your browser. It finds the Helper on its own
  and downloads go directly to your Downloads folder.


IF MACOS SAYS IT CANNOT VERIFY THE DEVELOPER

  Right-click "YouPiper Helper" in Applications, choose Open, then confirm.
  You only have to do this the first time.


TO REMOVE IT

  Double-click "Uninstall YouPiper Helper" in this window.
  Your downloaded files are never touched.


WHAT IT INCLUDES

  YouPiper Helper bundles everything it needs, so you do not have to
  install anything else. See the Licenses folder inside the application
  (right-click > Show Package Contents > Contents > Resources > Licenses)
  for the third-party software it includes and the licences that cover it.


PRIVACY AND NETWORK

  The Helper listens only on this computer's own loopback interface, on a
  single port. Nothing on your network or the internet can reach it. It
  does not create an account, collect analytics or send any usage data.
